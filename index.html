NITPIC calendar 🗓️

A calendar full of cool community events for Non-binary, Intersex, and Trans People In Cardiff ✨

Open this calendar in your own app... (iCal)

...or add it manually (Google Calendar)

Using a computer, navigate to calendar.google.com:

  1. Next to "Other calendars", click the +.
  2. Choose "From URL".
  3. Paste the subscription address below.
  4. Click "Add calendar".

Includes all groups, regardless of filters
Calendar apps check for updates periodically, so changes may take a while to appear
